# Yen (Yen-Yi) Huang — Product Design Portfolio

Personal portfolio site showcasing product design case studies, built and shipped end-to-end by me — from Figma to code to deployment.

🔗 **Live site:** [yen-portfolio](https://yenyihuang.com)

## About

I'm a Product Designer working across AI products and fintech. This site replaces a previous Squarespace portfolio with a custom-built Next.js site, designed in Figma and hand-coded (with AI-assisted development via Cursor).

### Case Studies

- **StockNews.AI** — 0→1 product design for an AI-generated financial news platform. Focused on trust signals for AI content, resulting in a 58% increase in conversion and a 25% reduction in engineer handoff cycles.
- **Design System** — A three-phase case study on building and scaling a design system into a React/Storybook component library.
- **Wondera** — Generative AI mobile app design, contributing to an 18% increase in DAU.

Plus a "More Work" carousel of additional projects and an About section.
